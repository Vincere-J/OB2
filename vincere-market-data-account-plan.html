Market Data and Account Plans on NinjaTrader

NinjaTrader changed how market data works: as long as your live account is funded, even at the minimum, the CME Level 1 data bundle is included at no extra cost. This guide also covers the separate account plan choice, which affects your per trade commissions, not your market data.

If you wish to see the walkthrough video, you can watch it here. Otherwise, the steps below have you covered. Your progress is saved on this device.

Tick each step as you go.

  1. 1

    Fund your account first

    0:00 to 0:55

    The CME Bundle Level 1 market data package used to require a separate paid subscription, around $12 to $16 a month. Now it is included automatically once your live NinjaTrader account has any funded balance. If your account is not funded yet, market data will not show as active.

    1. Complete the funding steps from the Fund Your Account guide first.
    2. Once your live account shows a funded balance, CME Level 1 data is included at no extra charge.
  2. 2

    Check that your market data is active

    0:55 to 1:35

    You can confirm your data package status from your account settings, so you are not left guessing whether it is turned on.

    1. Click your initials or profile icon, then go to Settings.
    2. Open the Plans tab and scroll down to the Market Data section.
    3. Look for the CME Bundle Level 1 line. It should show as Included or Free with a price of $0.00 per month once your account is funded.
  3. 3

    Review your account plan options

    1:35 to 2:20

    The account plan is a separate choice from market data. It does not affect whether you have data access, it changes your per trade commission rate. NinjaTrader offers three tiers: Free, Monthly ($99/month), and Lifetime ($1,499 one time, or in four payments).

    1. Click your initials, go to Settings, then Plans, then Account Plan, then Change Plan.
    2. Compare the three tiers: Live Trading Free (no cost, highest commissions), Live Trading Monthly ($99/month, reduced commissions), and Live Trading Lifetime ($1,499 one time or four payments, lowest commissions).
    3. You can pay at checkout with a card, or have it deducted from your funded account.
  4. 4

    Pick the plan that fits how long you will trade

    2:20 to end

    The lower commission rates on Monthly and Lifetime plans add up quickly given typical algo trade volume. Over a year of trading, the savings on commissions can exceed the cost of the plan itself.

    1. If you plan to trade for 12 months or more, or you see this as a long term investment vehicle, the Lifetime plan is recommended, since the commission savings alone typically make it back within 12 months.
    2. If you are not ready to commit, start with Monthly and upgrade later.
    3. The Free plan works, but is not recommended once you are trading real size, since the per trade commissions are the highest of the three tiers.
    Micro and non-micro commissions differ by plan. Compare the exact per contract commission shown on the Change Plan screen for your instruments before deciding.

  5. Market data and plan are set

    With market data confirmed and an account plan selected, you are ready to move on to connecting your account and, if your capital supports it, requesting sub-accounts.
