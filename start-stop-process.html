Cash Account Startup & Shutdown Process

ⓘAll times in ET. Follow the steps below to start or shut down your cash algo setup safely.
1
Startup Daily or after a shutdown
By 8:30a ET
as early as 7:30a ET
  1. Connect to your VPS by clicking the Remote Desktop shortcut (or the Windows App on Mac / Apple).
  2. Once the VPS loads in another window, double-click NinjaTrader to launch.
  3. Go to the Connections menu > Live.
  4. Verify charts are running (moving candlesticks).
  5. Check the Strategies tab: all strategy boxes should be green / true.
  6. Enable all strategies before 8:30a ET (as early as 7:30a ET). Any earlier can cause issues.
  7. You’re ready to trade. Leave NinjaTrader running.
Quick note: if NinjaTrader is already running (you did not shut down), just go to Connections > Live and continue from step 3.

2
If you don’t shut down NinjaTrader
★ Preferred for many users
  1. Ensure all strategy boxes in the Strategies tab are turned off.
  2. Go to the Connections menu > Live.
  3. NinjaTrader stays running; you can jump back in anytime.
  4. No need to restart unless required.
